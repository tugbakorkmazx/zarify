## Scope & Mode

Persuade. The platform landing page (`/`): couples deciding to build an invitation. Primary action: start (register / invitation details).

## Audience, Job, Action, Proof, Constraints

- **Audience:** a couple planning a wedding, browsing on phone or laptop, comparing invitation options.
- **Job:** see what Zarify makes, trust it's well made, see it's self-serve and bilingual with RSVP tracking.
- **Action:** "Davetiye Oluşturmaya Başla" (register or details form); secondary: browse templates.
- **Proof:** the live template itself, rendered in miniature from the real product. No testimonials, customer names, prices, or press exist — none may be invented.
- **Constraints:** TR/EN; auth-aware nav (logged in / admin); legal links in footer.

## Chosen Direction & Memorable Moment

**Herbaryum — vintage editorial botanical** (shared with the guest invitation; see its brief). Memorable moment: the landing is the magazine's own cover — the headline as masthead, the live invitation tipped into a collage on an olive field, pieces laying down one after another on load.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Zarify presents itself as the publisher of the periodical its invitations are issues of — the landing is the house cover and masthead, not a SaaS hero with feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Same as guest invitation: ivory paper, olive fields, wine accent, soot rules; Gloock / EB Garamond small caps / no script except the signature in the footer mark.

STORY: Cover (offer + action + the real template in a collage) → contents-page "how it works" with large wine numerals → the template plate → three columns of notes on why → olive damask closing call to action → colophon footer.

LAYOUT: every section sits on a centre axis, like a title page — no left-aligned editorial columns. FIRST VIEWPORT: wordmark centred between two short navs over a double hairline; eyebrow, Gloock headline, body and two actions centred under it; then a centred olive panel holding the cream invitation miniature (bee on its corner), a swallow flying in from the left, and the weeping willow standing on the cream to the right (wide screens only).

FORM: brief-pinned vintage editorial botanical; seed key `8123077a`; same raises as the guest invitation (margin apparatus captions; cover-only grid break).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
